MY TOP-UP — PERSONAL VERSION
1. Open index.html in Chrome to test.
2. Choose friend, service and package. The app shows USD and an NPR estimate.
3. Review before opening the official checkout.
4. History and receipts are stored locally on this phone only.
5. It never stores card number, CVV, OTP, PIN or bank password.
6. For a true Android Home Screen app/WebAPK, Chrome needs the app served from HTTPS (or a supported installable web-app host). A ZIP opened directly from Downloads may not show "Install app".
7. Prices in this app are reference package prices and may not match the final checkout. The NPR rate is a visible estimate and should be updated when needed.

V6: TikTok now has visible Gallery and Camera QR buttons. Camera uses Android's file/capture picker, which works even when the app is opened from a local content:// file. QR images are decoded locally when BarcodeDetector is available.
